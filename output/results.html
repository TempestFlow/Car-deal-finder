How deal scores are calculated

Score Factors (0–100)

FactorWeightDescription
■ Price 40% How far below median market price for same make/model/year. 50% below = 100 pts.
■ Mileage 30% Expected 15,000 km/year. Near-zero mileage = 100. Double expected = 0. Suspiciously low mileage on old cars capped at 90.
■ Freshness 15% Decays 1 pt/day from 100. Hits 0 at 100 days. +15 bonus if a price drop was detected.
■ Seller 15% Private = 100 (no dealer markup). Dealer = 40.

Score Tiers

≥ 70 — Great deal. Noticeably below market, fresh listing, and/or low mileage.
40–69 — Fair deal. Around market average. Worth a look.
< 40 — Below average. Overpriced, high mileage, or stale listing.

Formula & Adjustments

score = price×0.40 + mileage×0.30
      + freshness×0.15 + seller×0.15

+ 5–10 reliability bonus (Toyota, Honda, Lexus…)
+ 3–8   TA bonus (>6 months left)
+ 0–3   TA small bonus (3–6 months left)
− 7–10 TA penalty (expired or no TA)
− 30    damage penalty (accident / body damage)
Reliable cars reference guide

Well-proven models — historically low ownership costs & high longevity

Toyota Corolla Legendary
  • Consistently top-ranked in long-term reliability surveys worldwide
  • Simple, over-engineered 1.6–2.0 petrol engines rarely fail
  • Huge parts availability keeps repair costs minimal
  • Rust resistance better than most European rivals
Toyota Camry Legendary
  • 2.5 hybrid drivetrain routinely exceeds 400,000 km without major work
  • Toyota’s flagship saloon; engineering tolerances are exceptionally tight
  • Low depreciation signals strong residual trust from the market
Lexus IS / ES Legendary
  • Toyota mechanicals with premium build quality — best of both worlds
  • Hybrid variants have near-zero transmission issues vs. German rivals
  • Dealer service records very common, adding confidence at purchase
Honda Civic (2006–2021) Excellent
  • 1.8 i-VTEC and 2.0 engines are among the longest-lasting 4-cylinders made
  • Simple suspension geometry means low wear even on rough Lithuanian roads
  • High owner satisfaction in JD Power studies for 15+ consecutive years
Mazda3 / Mazda6 Excellent
  • SkyActiv engines avoid turbochargers, eliminating a major failure point
  • Corrosion protection improved significantly from 2013 onward
  • Consistently low repair frequency in European consumer reports
Skoda Octavia (2013+) Great
  • VW Group MQB platform — well-proven, parts everywhere in Lithuania
  • 1.6 TDI diesel is a workhorse engine with 300,000+ km potential
  • Practical estate body popular with local buyers, strong resale value
  • Cheaper to maintain than equivalent VW/Audi by 20–30%
Subaru Outback / Forester Great
  • Symmetrical AWD standard on all variants — no torque-split failures
  • Boxer engines run cool and last well when serviced on schedule
  • Head gaskets on pre-2012 EJ engines are the one known weak point — check service history
Toyota Land Cruiser Legendary
  • 1HZ and 1VD-FTV diesel engines are routinely driven 500,000+ km
  • Body-on-frame construction makes structural repairs straightforward
  • Preferred by humanitarian organizations for guaranteed field reliability
  • Strong demand keeps resale value high even at high mileage
Volvo V70 / XC70 (2007–2016) Great
  • D5 diesel engine is tough and well-understood by Baltic mechanics
  • Excellent passive safety ratings add real-world value on rural roads
  • Estate format very practical; large used-parts market in Lithuania
  • Avoid the early DPF and DSTC modules — all others are low-risk
